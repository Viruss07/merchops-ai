# MerchOps AI — Partner & Judge Demo Script (3–4 Minutes)

> **PayPal AI Hackathon 2026**  
> **Core Architecture Principle**: *"AI proposes, rules decide. AI must never independently move money."*

---

## Demo Overview

Small merchants run on tight margins. When a major supplier bill arrives before customers pay their invoices, merchants face overdrafts, fee penalties, or payroll crunches.

**MerchOps AI** solves this by pairing **deterministic cash-flow rules** with **human-in-the-loop AI assistance**.

### Initial Seed State
The scenario runs on a deterministic clock: `DEMO_AS_OF` defaults to **2026-11-03** and is env-overridable. Invoice aging is derived from `dueDate`, so the numbers below do not drift with the real calendar.
- **Cleared Cash**: `$4,200.00`
- **Safety Reserve Floor**: `$2,000.00`
- **Overdue Receivable**: Apex Corp — `$1,500.00` (due `2026-10-22`; 12 days past due)
- **Upcoming Payable**: Acme Supplies — `$3,000.00` (due `2026-11-13`; Day 10)

---

## Act 1: Impending Cash Shortfall (Runway & Forecast Tab)

**Time**: 0:00 – 0:45  
**Screen**: `Runway & Forecast` Tab

1. **Point out the Initial Balances**:
   - Point to the KPI card: **Current Cleared Cash** is `$4,200.00`.
   - Point to the **Safety Reserve Floor**: `$2,000.00`.
2. **Observe the Day 10 Breach Alert**:
   - Notice the prominent red warning banner: **"Projected Cash Shortfall of $800.00 on Day 10"**.
   - Look at the 30-day trajectory bar chart: Day 1 through 9 are blue (safe). On Day 10 (**2026-11-13**), when Acme Supplies' `$3,000.00` bill matures, the bar drops to **`$1,200.00`** in bright red.
   - It breaches the `$2,000.00` dashed reserve floor by exactly **`$800.00`**.
3. **Key Talking Point**:
   > *"Notice that this forecast is not generated by an LLM guessing numbers. It is computed by a 100% deterministic calculation engine in exact integer cents. Zero math hallucinations."*

---

## Act 2: Guardrail Enforcement (Payouts Tab)

**Time**: 0:45 – 1:30  
**Screen**: `Payouts (AP)` Tab

1. **Navigate to the Payouts tab**:
   - Observe the pending bill: **Acme Supplies ($3,000.00)**.
2. **Examine the Reserve Policy Guardrail**:
   - The top banner alerts: *"Acme Supplies disbursement ($3,000.00) blocked by Cash Reserve Policy"*.
   - In the table, the **Reserve Check** column displays:
     `Breaches Floor (Leaves $1,200.00 • Shortfall -$800.00)`.
3. **Click "Review (Blocked)"**:
   - The **Two-Step Payout Authorization Modal** opens.
   - Point out that the modal calculates the exact liquidity impact: `$4,200.00 - $3,000.00 = $1,200.00`, breaching the `$2,000.00` floor by `$800.00`.
   - Show that the authorization checkbox is **disabled** and the button states **"Blocked by Reserve Floor"**.
   - Even if someone attempts to bypass the UI via raw API calls, `/api/payouts/authorize` strictly returns **HTTP 403 `RESERVE_BREACH_BLOCKED`**.
4. **Key Talking Point**:
   > *"The platform protects working capital automatically. Outbound payments that would compromise liquidity are locked until the shortfall is resolved."*

---

## Act 3: AI-Assisted Accelerated Collection (Collections Tab)

**Time**: 1:30 – 2:30  
**Screen**: `Collections (AR)` Tab

1. **Click "Resolve Shortfall in Collections"** (or click the Collections tab):
   - Notice the overdue receivable banner: **Apex Corp — $1,500.00 (12 days past due)**.
2. **Click "Draft Reminder"**:
   - The slide-over drawer opens.
   - An AI-tailored reminder email is presented, customized for a 12-day overdue invoice.
   - It references the invoice number (`INV-2026-089`), exact amount (`$1,500.00`), and includes the PayPal payer link (a real Invoicing v2 `recipient_view_url` once the invoice is created with sandbox keys; a simulated link otherwise).
3. **Show Merchant Oversight**:
   - Edit the draft subject or body to demonstrate full merchant editing control.
   - Click **"Approve Reminder Draft"**.
   - Status updates to **"Draft Approved & Staged"**. Approval is persisted server-side at `POST /api/reminders/approve` and an audit entry is written. No outbound email is sent.
4. **Simulate Customer Payment**:
   - Click **"Simulate Pay"** on the Apex Corp row.
   - The confirmation modal displays: *"Notice: This is a local ledger demonstration simulating customer settlement. No actual money movement or PayPal live transactions have occurred."*
   - Click **"Confirm Settlement"**. The settlement is attributed to `MERCHANT` (not a PayPal webhook; this collection demo does not exercise the payout webhook receiver).
5. **Observe Instant Reconciliation**:
   - Apex Corp status updates to **Paid**.
   - Notice the **Cleared Cash** in the header jumps from `$4,200.00` to **`$5,700.00`** (`$4,200 + $1,500`).
6. **Key Talking Point**:
   > *"AI proposed the reminder; the merchant approved it. The resulting customer collection immediately restores the merchant's working capital."*

---

## Act 4: Payout Unlocked & Executed (Payouts Tab)

**Time**: 2:30 – 3:15  
**Screen**: `Payouts (AP)` & `Runway` Tabs

1. **Return to the Runway & Forecast tab**:
   - The red Day-10 breach has vanished!
   - The banner updates to **"30-Day Working Capital Trajectory Secured"** with a **`+$700.00`** safety buffer.
2. **Switch to the Payouts tab**:
   - The banner is now green: **"Working capital restored — Acme Supplies ($3,000.00) ready for authorization"**.
   - The table shows: `Reserve Safe (Leaves $2,700.00 • Buffer +$700.00)`.
   - The **"Authorize Payout"** button is now active.
3. **Click "Authorize Payout"**:
   - The **Two-Step Authorization Modal** opens.
   - Step 1: Merchant reviews recipient (`receivables@acmesupplies.test`), amount (`$3,000.00`), and projected post-payout balance (`$2,700.00`).
   - Notice the deterministic per-bill **PayPal-Request-Id**. It is tracked in-process through `APPROVED -> IN_FLIGHT -> DISBURSED`; replaying the same request returns the stored result instead of paying twice.
   - Step 2: Merchant checks the explicit confirmation box.
   - Click **"Authorize & Disburse $3,000.00"**.
4. **Execution & Settlement (SIMULATED)**:
   - Status changes to **Paid & Settled** on the local simulation rail (the default).
   - Cleared cash updates to **`$2,700.00`** (`$5,700 - $3,000`), remaining safely `$700.00` above the `$2,000.00` reserve floor.
   - The live PayPal Sandbox rail is implemented but disabled unless the server env `PAYPAL_PAYOUTS_ENABLED=true`; the browser cannot enable it, and live dispatch is unverified.
5. **Key Talking Point**:
   > *"No money moves until a human explicitly authorizes it. Idempotency keys protect every execution, while reserve rules ensure the merchant stays solvent."*

## Act 5: Audit Trail & Wrap-Up (Audit Trail Tab)

**Time**: 3:15 – 3:45  
**Screen**: `Audit Trail` Tab

1. **Review the chronological audit log**:
   - `PAYOUT_DISBURSED_SIMULATED`: Acme Supplies payout (`-$3,000.00`).
   - `INVOICE_PAID_SIMULATED`: Apex Corp settlement (`+$1,500.00`).
   - `REMINDER_DRAFT_APPROVED`: Merchant approved AI reminder draft.
   - `PAYOUT_BLOCKED_RESERVE_BREACH`: Initial unsafe payout attempt blocked by policy.
2. **Honesty & Transparency Callout**:
   - Point to the in-memory demo notice:
     *"This audit trail is an in-memory array with UUID IDs. It is not an immutable production ledger and resets on server restart. There is no PostgreSQL persistence and no authentication. The PayPal webhook receiver is implemented but unverified against a live delivery."*
3. **Closing Summary**:
   > *"MerchOps AI empowers small merchants to anticipate cash crunches, accelerate receivables with AI drafts, and disburse payouts safely under reserve protection. Throughout the entire system, one non-negotiable rule holds: **AI proposes, rules decide. AI never independently moves money.**"*

## Quick Reset for Next Demo

To reset the demo back to the Day-10 breach scenario at any time:
- Click the **"Reset Demo"** button in the top navigation bar (the API requires `{confirm:true}` plus a same-origin request; in production, a matching `x-demo-admin-token` when `DEMO_ADMIN_TOKEN` is set).
- The balance immediately returns to `$4,200.00`, Apex Corp resets to Overdue, Acme Supplies resets to Pending, and the Day-10 reserve breach alert reappears.
